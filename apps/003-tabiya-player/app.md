Tabiya Player is Tabiya's companion, for what comes after the opening. You play a full game
against the Stockfish chess engine.

Afterwards, a review names every slip and the move the engine preferred. The review uses
the same numbers the evaluation bar showed during the game. So you are judged by exactly
what you were watching, and a position the bar called level cannot turn into a blunder
afterwards.

The installer is not signed with a code signing certificate, so Windows warns that the
publisher is unknown and asks whether to continue. That warning is expected.
