Take territories, hold whole regions for extra armies, trade cards for reinforcements, and
knock every other player out.

There are three hand-drawn maps: Australia, Australia in detail, and the World. You can
also draw a map of your own in the built-in editor. Eight kinds of computer opponent each
play their own way, from Reckless to Optimus, whose numbers were found by having it play
against itself.

Several people can share one iPad and take turns. Add it to the Home Screen and it plays
like an app, even with the network off. After a game, a replay shows how the map changed,
move by move.
