DataCrafter is my main product: an automated workbench for analysing data and producing
reports. You load your data, model it, classify it, aggregate it and publish it as one
repeatable run. Then you put that run on a schedule and leave it.

These pictures show the month-end close for a made-up bakery with two shops. Every name in
them is invented.

It has a desktop client, a web client and an Excel add-in. The releases are public and the
source is private. The [DataCrafter website](https://www.datacrafter.net) has the full story.
